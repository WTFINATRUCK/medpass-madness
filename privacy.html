Privacy Policy

MedPass Madness · Last updated November 1, 2026

MedPass Madness is a single-player game. We built it so that it does not collect personal information.

What the game stores

Your game progress (stars, coins, upgrades, best scores and the sound setting) is saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app or clearing its data erases it. You can make a backup code in Settings; that code stays wherever you choose to paste it.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-ins, names or email addresses.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising IDs or third-party SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access.
  • No data is sold or shared.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Not medical advice

MedPass Madness is for entertainment and general learning. It is not clinical guidance. Always follow your facility's policies and a licensed professional's direction.

Changes & contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page. Questions: open an issue at github.com/WTFINATRUCK/medpass-madness.
